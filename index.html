01 — about

From the loading dock to the dashboard

I've spent the last 15 years focused on fresh produce — buying, selling, and keeping product moving from grower to shelf. At Westfalia Fruit I support the North American sales team, and increasingly the questions I'm asked are data questions: pricing trends, volumes, freight.

So I went back to school. I completed The Tech Academy's AI Software Developer Bootcamp, building a foundation in Python, SQL, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and I'm applying it daily — turning spreadsheets and portal exports into insight the sales team can act on.

Off the clock: golf, running, live music, and landscape photography around the Pacific Northwest.
