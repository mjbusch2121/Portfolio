About

I am currently enrolled in The Tech Academy's AI Developer program, studying core technologies including HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Python, with a long-term focus on Python and SQL for data analytics—a learning journey that will continue for many years ahead. My goal is to blend hands‑on industry experience with adaptable, data‑driven problem‑solving to support sustainable practices and strengthen the connection between growers, communities, and the food systems we all rely on.


I am also a golfer, runner, photographer, traveler, and live music concert-goer!


I look forward to this learning journey and can’t wait to keep growing, adapting, and exploring new possibilities wherever my skills can make an impact.
